# 2. Choose a strategy

> Offline copy of the AlgoCloud help article (fetched 2026-08-07).

AlgoCloud is a cloud trading platform that executes your own algo strategies, so you need some algo strategy / strategies to be able to trade with AlgoCloud.

Note

AlgoCloud is a trading platform allowing you to trade your own trading strategies. We are not an investment advisor, we don't offer ready-made strategies for you to use. All the examples on AlgoCloud website and application are for educational purposes only.

## How to get strategies to trade

There are several ways to get trading strategies for you to trade:

## 1. Environment of partners

We have a growing environment of partners who will provide AlgoCloud content from training courses to strategies ready to be traded.

The first one of such partners is AlgoHubb - they offer a range of strategies to choose from.

We are always looking for new partners - if you are interested in becoming our partner in one of these areas please don't hesitate to contact us

## 2. Use AlgoCloud examples

You can see different examples of single-stock and stockpicker strategies in the AlgoCloud App.

Please note that all the examples are provided for educational purposes, demonstrating various types of possible strategy edges.

But you can definitely pick one or more of the examples and run it on demo account on paper money - to get an understanding how live trading works in AlgoCloud.

## 3. Create your own strategy

Our built-in no-code Algo Creator / Editor enables even non-programmers to construct their own algo trading strategy.

A lot of robust trading strategies have surprisingly simple logic and rules - just check the examples for a few concepts.

It is not difficult to test your idea of trading rules in Algo Editor, you'll see if it works almost immediately with an integrated backtest engine.

This way you can also test various ideas and come up with something unique.
