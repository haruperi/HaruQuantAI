# Special Offer bonuses — how to get them

> Offline copy of the AlgoCloud help article (fetched 2026-08-07).

If you signed up through algocloud.com/special-offer, your package includes several bonus strategies and a custom symbol list. This article explains what the bonus contains and how you receive it.

# What's in the Special Offer

-

Sector rotation strategy (Stockpicker) with the custom sector ETF symbol list

-

Gap overnight strategy

-

Pullback RSI strategy

-

Additional fast-action bonuses for early buyers

Exact contents can vary — see the landing page you signed up from.

# How you receive the bonuses

Bonus files are delivered by email, not automatically loaded into your AlgoCloud account. After signup you should receive an email from AlgoCloud support with:

-

Google Drive links to the bonus strategies (.sqx files)

-

Documentation (Google Docs) for each strategy

-

A custom symbol list for the sector rotation strategy, typically:

XLK.D, XLE.D, XLV.D, XLY.D, XLF.D, XLI.D, XLU.D, XLB.D, XLRE.D, XLC.D

# How to load a bonus strategy into AlgoCloud

-

Download the .sqx file from the Google Drive link.

-

In AlgoCloud, go to My strategies.

-

Click Import and select the downloaded .sqx file.

-

The strategy now appears in your list — you can run a backtest or deploy it.

For sector rotation, also create the custom symbol list in Stock groups using the sector ETFs above.

# "I don't see the bonuses in my account"

That's expected — the bonuses are not added to your account automatically. They come by email.

If you didn't receive the email within 24 hours of signup:

-

Check your spam / promotions folder.

-

Contact [email protected] with the email you signed up with and the date of purchase — we'll resend the bonus pack.
