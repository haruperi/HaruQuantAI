# Introduction to AlgoCloud

> Offline copy of the AlgoCloud help article (fetched 2026-08-07).

Welcome to AlgoCloud (AC for short).

AlgoCloud is an online cloud trading platform with one goal - to make algo trading stock simple and profitable!

It allows you to deploy your own stock algo strategies on the cloud and trade them automatically on live or demo accounts of a supported broker.

[image]

## What we mean by simple

In AlgoCloud we created a new, no-code class of trading tools to open the world of algo trading also for non programmers, and to make live trading simple and hassle-free, without the need for things like maintaining your own VPS servers, keeping your trading platform running and so on.

## What we mean by profitable

AlgoCloud is unique in giving you a possibility to trade Stockpicker type of strategies without any programming. These are strategies that trade groups of stocks, and offer potentially better performance at lower risk compared to classic Buy & Hold approach.

Of course, your performance will vary depending on your own strategy selection, its configuration and your actions.

AlgoCloud is a trading platform, not an investment advisor, and past performance of any trading strategy is no guarantee of future results.
