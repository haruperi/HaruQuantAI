## Frequently asked questions

## What markets can I trade with AlgoCloud?

AlgoCloud currently supports US equities and ETFs on a daily timeframe. The exact offer of stocks and ETFs depend also on the connected broker.

## Can AlgoCloud be used for other assets than equities?

No, AlgoCloud is targeted to equities and we don’t plan to support other assets in short term.

## How are my strategies protected?

Strategies you create on AlgoCloud are your own and we take your intellectual property seriously:

- trading strategies are stored encrypted per user

- we employ a variety of security technologies and procedures designed to help prevent unauthorized disclosure of, access to and use of information that you have on AlgoCloud.

- you are also strongly advised to use Two-Factor Authentication for login.

## Which brokers do you support?

We are working on connections to as many brokers as possible.

List of currently supported brokers:

- eToro

- Alpaca.markets – commission-free US based broker.

- Tradestation

- Interactive Brokers (US Citizens only)

- Robomarkets

## Do you plan to support other brokers?

Yes, our goal is to be integrated with every major broker.

We are also actively communicating with other major brokers – Tradestation, TDAmeritrade / Charles Schwab, eToro, Trading212, and others.

## Do you plan to support other timeframes (intraday) or other stock exchanges?

Not right now, but it is a possibility in the future.

## What are the conditions of your Money back guarantee?

There are no conditions, no questions asked. If you are not satisfied with your purchase let us know within 14 days and we’ll give you a full refund.
