Configure automatic filter

Automatic filter should be used to dismiss strategies that are obviously wrong, for example they don't produce any trades, or trades are ambiguous and cannot be backtested properly.
It is highly recommended to keep all automatic filters turned on!

Threshold values for these conditions are: at least 10% or max 100 of the trades must be bad in order to mark startegy as bad.
For ambiguous trades the threshold is decreased to 5% or max 50 trades.
