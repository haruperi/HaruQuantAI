# Fractional shares

> Offline copy of the AlgoCloud help article (fetched 2026-08-07).

Fractional shares support means that the broker allows you to buy or sell a fraction of a stock - for example only 0.02 stock.

Its availability depends on the broker and the symbol - it is up to broker discretion if it supports fractional shares on stocks and on which symbols exactly.

Please check the updated documentation for your broker, at the time of writing this article the situation is as follows.

## InteractiveBrokers

Documentation link: www.interactivebrokers.com/en/trading/fractional-trading.php

-

supports all order types

-

-

## Alpaca

Documentation: alpaca.markets/deprecated/docs/trading-on-alpaca/fractional-trading/

-

supports Market and day order types

-

Not all assets are fractionable

## Tradestation

Tradestation currently does not offer fractional shares.
