# Overview of supported brokers

AlgoCloud is broker-agnostic, it can work with all brokers that provide a public API or a way of connection.

We support a few major brokers in both USA and worldwide, with goal of adding more brokers over time.

Right now, we fully support these brokers:

-

eToro

-

Alpaca.markets - commission-free and algo-friendly stocks and crypto broker based in the USA, it accepts clients from all over the world.

Fully supported in AlgoCloud for real money trading.

-

Tradestation - respected US broker offering a wide selection of assets. It's one disadvantage with AlgoCloud is that it is really suitable only for accounts over $25,000 USD.

Brokers in beta testing:

-

InteractiveBrokers - low commission broker with a huge list of tradable assets.

**Available only to US-residents

Note

Interactive Brokers connection in AlgoCloud is currently available for US residents ONLY. This is a legal requirement from Interactive Brokers. We are working with them to enable usage of IB for clients world-wide.

-

RoboMarkets - low commission European broker. Connection to Robomarkets is available for beta testing.

-

Trading212
