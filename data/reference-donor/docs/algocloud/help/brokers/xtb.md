# XTB

> Offline copy of the AlgoCloud help article (fetched 2026-08-07).

XTB provides an easy to use API for trading, and connecting this broker to AC is simple.

## Link account for XTB

## Step 1 - Prepare your XTB credentials

You will need XTB Account ID and password to create connection to this broker in AlgoCloud.

Password is the same password that is used to log in to XTB xStation, and Account ID can be found on the top right corner of the xStation after login.

[image]

​

​

## Step 2 - Link to broker in AC cloud

Once you have your Alpaca keys you can create a connection in AlgoCloud.

Go to Connected accounts screen. This is where you manage all your connections to brokers.

Click on Link account button.

[image]

This will open wizard that will lead you through the process.

The first step is to choose your broker from the list of supported ones. You can give your new connected account any name - it will be used internally in AlgoCloud.

[image]

The second step is specifying the connection.

Put your XTB Account ID and XTB account password.

[image]

The third step is (optionally) turn on Account protection.

[image]

After clicking Next the connection is created.

[image]

AlgoCloud will then try to log in to XTB API and retrieve basic information about your account like balance, equity, positions.
