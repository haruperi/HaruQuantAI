# Profile

> Offline copy of the AlgoCloud help article (fetched 2026-08-07).

The Profile screen is where you manage your AlgoCloud account — email, password, two-factor authentication, notifications and account deactivation.

# Profile Details

Here you can change your email and password and set up two-factor authentication (2FA).

[image]

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

2FA adds an extra security layer. To enable it:

-

Click Enable 2FA.

-

Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, Microsoft Authenticator, etc.).

-

Enter the 6-digit code from the app to confirm.

-

Save the recovery codes AlgoCloud displays — store them somewhere safe. These let you sign in if you lose your authenticator device.

Lost access to 2FA? Use one of your recovery codes at sign-in. If you've lost those too, contact [email protected] from the email on your account and we'll verify identity and reset 2FA for you.

# Deactivate account

If you want to stop using AlgoCloud, you can deactivate your account here. Deactivation:

-

Stops all running strategies.

-

Disconnects linked brokers.

-

Disables the account sign-in.

After clicking Deactivate, you will receive a confirmation email with a verification link. Click the link in the email to complete deactivation.

[image]

## Didn't receive the deactivation email?

-

Check your spam / promotions folder.

-

Make sure the email on your profile is current — if it's not, you won't get the link.

-

If you signed up with Apple Sign-In using Hide My Email, the confirmation goes to your private Apple relay address. Log in to appleid.apple.com to find it, or contact [email protected] and we'll process the deactivation manually after verifying your identity.
