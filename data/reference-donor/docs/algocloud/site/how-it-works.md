## What markets can I trade with AlgoCloud?

AlgoCloud currently supports US equities and ETFs on a daily timeframe. The exact offer of stocks and ETFs depend also on the connected broker.

## Can AlgoCloud be used for other assets than equities?

No, AlgoCloud is targeted to equities and we don’t plan to support other assets in short term.

## Which brokers do you support?

We fully support Alpaca.markets, which is a commission-free US based broker, TradeStation nad Interactive Brokers (US Citizens only).

We are working on connections for other brokers.

## Do you plan to support other brokers?

We are also actively communicating with other major brokers – Tradestation, TDAmeritrade / Charles Schwab, and others.

Our goal is to be integrated with every major broker.
